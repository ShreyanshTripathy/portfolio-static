## Design Context

### Users

Visitors are people who want to understand Shreyansh Tripathy as a person, see the work he has built, and follow the journey behind it. This includes potential collaborators, research supervisors, admissions reviewers, recruiters, peers, and curious visitors. Their central job is not merely to scan credentials; it is to discover what Shreyansh cares about, how he thinks, what he has attempted, and where he hopes to go next.

### Brand Personality

Ambitious, inventive, and human. The portfolio should feel like someone with large dreams who moves naturally between physics, technology, experimentation, and making. It should communicate curiosity, resilience, and forward momentum. The emotional reference is Tony Stark's restless inventor energy and confidence, expressed as an original personal identity rather than Marvel branding or a superhero imitation.

### Aesthetic Direction

A cinematic experimental workshop crossed with a thoughtful research notebook: precise instrumentation, technical annotations, physical materials, diagrams, prototypes, and moments of wonder. Preserve support for both dark and light themes, with the dark experience carrying more of the cinematic workshop atmosphere. Build on the existing oxide blue, wafer gold, IBM Plex Sans, Recursive, experimental imagery, and industrial details, while making the journey and aspirations more emotionally visible. Avoid generic developer neon, cyberpunk dashboards, excessive glassmorphism, superhero cosplay, and a conventional corporate résumé-template appearance.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the person and the dream; credentials and technologies should support the story rather than replace it.
2. Treat projects as experiments with questions, attempts, discoveries, setbacks, and outcomes—not interchangeable cards.
3. Make physics and technology tangible through instrument-like details, measured typography, real images, diagrams, and purposeful motion.
4. Create cinematic confidence through composition and pacing while keeping the voice candid, warm, and recognizably human.
5. Keep the site fast, accessible, responsive, and fully compatible with static GitHub Pages deployment.
